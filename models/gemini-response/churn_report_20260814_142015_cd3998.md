## Segment Overview
Cluster 1 is our largest and highest-risk customer segment, containing 15,315 total customers. It carries a critical risk category designation with a severe churn rate of 64.38% (9,860 churned vs. 5,455 retained customers).

## Churn Probability
- **Total Customers in Segment:** 15,315
- **Churned Customers:** 9,860
- **Churn Rate:** 64.38%
- **Stay Rate:** 35.62%
- **Root Cause Drivers:** Customers who churned had a significantly higher average churn risk score (1.0 vs 0.0 for stayers), fewer points in their wallet (638.79 vs 725.45), slightly higher age (26.09 vs 23.18 years), lower platform engagement (average time spent 152.34 vs 190.82 minutes), and a stark lack of membership (31.05% of churners had "No Membership" compared to just 1.52% of stayers).

## Risk Level
This is our most critical segment due to its massive volume of lost customers (9,860) and an alarming 64.38% churn rate. Immediate aggressive intervention is required for customers showing a high churn risk score, lacking active memberships, and exhibiting declining wallet points or engagement time.
