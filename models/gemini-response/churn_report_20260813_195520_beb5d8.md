## Segment Overview
Cluster 1 is a large customer segment totaling 15,315 customers categorized as "Risky," featuring a high concentration of users lacking formal membership and exhibiting lower engagement levels.

## Churn Probability
- **Total Customers:** 15,315
- **Churned Customers:** 9,860
- **Churn Rate:** 64.38%
- **Stay Rate:** 35.62%
- **Risk Level:** Risky

## Root Causes (with real numbers)
- **Churn Risk Score:** Customers who churned had a significantly higher churn risk score (average of 1.00) compared to those who stayed (average of 0.00).
- **Points in Wallet:** Churned customers held fewer loyalty points in their wallet (638.79 points) compared to those who stayed (725.45 points).
- **Age:** Churned customers were slightly older on average (26.09 years) compared to those who stayed (23.18 years).
- **Membership Category:** Lack of membership is a massive driver; 31.05% of churners had "No Membership," compared to only 1.52% of those who stayed.
- **Engagement (Avg Time Spent):** Churned customers spent less time on the platform (152.34 minutes) compared to those who stayed (190.82 minutes).

## Suggested Retention Actions
1. **Urgent Membership Conversion Campaigns:** Target non-members (who make up over 31% of churners) with aggressive, discounted introductory membership trials.
2. **Wallet Point Top-Ups:** Incentivize active participation by granting bonus loyalty points to bridge the gap for users with lower wallet balances (under 640 points).
3. **Re-engagement Triggers for Low Time-Spent:** Implement automated push notifications and email nudges when user platform session time drops below 150 minutes.
4. **Targeted Age-Group Messaging:** Tailor product recommendations and communication tones specifically suited for customers around the 26-year-old demographic.
5. **Early Warning System Intervention:** Automatically flag any user whose calculated `churn_risk_score` hits 1.0 for immediate customer success outreach.
6. **In-App Onboarding Refresher:** Provide interactive guides for users showing declining platform engagement to demonstrate hidden value.
7. **Personalized Discount Vouchers:** Offer expiring-soon discounts redeemable using wallet points to encourage immediate spending.
8. **Feedback Surveys on Drop-off:** Trigger quick pulse surveys when a customer's session length decreases week-over-week to identify friction points.
9. **Exclusive Non-Member Perks:** Introduce low-barrier, free-tier perks to hook non-members before they consider leaving permanently.
10. **VIP Win-Back Offers:** Deploy dedicated customer success agents with special incentive packages for high-value profiles identified within this risky segment.
