Here is a simple breakdown of why customers churn in each cluster based on the latest data:

---

### **Cluster 0**
## Segment Overview
A large segment of standard-value shoppers where churn is heavily tied to low reward balances and basic memberships.
## Churn Rate
59.99%
## Root Causes (with real numbers)
* **Points in Wallet:** Customers who left had fewer loyalty points compared to those who stayed (**624.95 vs 723.81 points**).
* **Membership Type:** **37.71%** of churned customers held a Basic Membership, compared to only **1.78%** of those who stayed.
* **Login Frequency:** Customers who churned logged in slightly less often (**17.92 days** between logins vs **16.92 days** for stayers).

---

### **Cluster 1**
## Segment Overview
Our largest customer group, skewed younger, where a lack of engagement and missing paid memberships drive people away.
## Churn Rate
64.38%
## Root Causes (with real numbers)
* **Age:** Customers who churned tended to be slightly older on average (**26.09 years vs 23.18 years**).
* **Membership Type:** **31.05%** of churned users had **No Membership**, compared to just **1.52%** of those who stayed.
* **Time Spent:** Customers who left spent significantly less time engaging on the platform (**152.34 minutes vs 190.82 minutes** for stayers).
* **Points in Wallet:** Churned users held fewer wallet points (**638.79 vs 725.45 points**).

---

### **Cluster 2**
## Segment Overview
A smaller, mid-value customer segment where lower transaction values and basic tiers cause individuals to drop off.
## Churn Rate
57.03%
## Root Causes (with real numbers)
* **Transaction Value:** Customers who churned made lower-value purchases than those who stayed (**$25,852.87 vs $30,740.44**).
* **Membership Type:** **37.62%** of churned users had a **Basic Membership**, compared to only **1.61%** of stayers.
* **Points in Wallet:** Churned users had lower loyalty balances (**633.49 vs 735.25 points**).
* **Login Frequency:** Customers who left logged in less frequently (**17.45 days vs 15.49 days**).

---

### **Cluster 3**
## Segment Overview
Our healthiest and most valuable customer segment (lowest churn overall), where churn is triggered by low transaction spending and a total lack of membership perks.
## Churn Rate
24.07%
## Root Causes (with real numbers)
* **Transaction Value:** The biggest differentiator here—customers who left spent substantially less per transaction (**$41,024.52 vs $61,526.52** for stayers).
* **Membership Type:** **35.86%** of churned customers had **No Membership**, compared to just **0.48%** of those who stayed.
* **Feedback:** **18.68%** of customers who stayed praised the platform for having a *"User Friendly Website"*, whereas none of the churned customers gave this feedback.
