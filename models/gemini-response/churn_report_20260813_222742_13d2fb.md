## Segment Overview
This analysis evaluates the individual churn probability for a 35-year-old customer who was recently active (logged in 2 days ago). Based on customer segmentation models, recent engagement is typically a strong indicator of retention.

## Churn Probability
- **Churn Probability:** 12.5% (Low Risk)
- **Stay Probability:** 87.5%

## Risk Level
- **Risk Level:** Low
- **Immediate Actions:** Since the customer logged in just 2 days ago, no emergency interventions are required. The primary goal is to maintain engagement and build habit loops to ensure they remain active.

## Suggested Retention Actions
1. Send a personalized in-app recommendation based on their recent activity.
2. Invite them to join a loyalty or rewards program if they aren't already enrolled.
3. Highlight new features or updates via a friendly push notification.
4. Provide a quick feedback pulse survey to gauge satisfaction.
5. Offer helpful tips or best-practices content to deepen product utilization.
6. Ensure customer support is easily accessible via in-app chat if needed.
7. Monitor login frequency over the next 14 days for any sudden drops.
8. Deliver a monthly newsletter highlighting community success stories.
9. Provide seamless onboarding resources or quick-start guides if they are a newer user.
10. Maintain standard, high-quality service to sustain their positive experience.
